**Effective date:** November 17, 2026

## About this website

Reno Business Property provides commercial real estate information and a way to contact Peter Larsen, CCIM, at Dickson Commercial Group. Brokerage services are provided through Dickson Commercial Group.

**Website administrator and contact:** Peter Larsen, CCIM, a Nevada-licensed salesperson affiliated with Dickson Commercial Group. Reno Business Property is the name of this website, not a separately licensed real estate brokerage.

This policy describes how information connected with renobusinessproperty.com may be collected, used, stored, and shared.

## Information we collect

**Information you provide:** When you contact us by phone or email about a property or space question, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, business name, intended property use, property address or preferred area, approximate square footage, budget, desired timing, and anything else you include.

**Newsletter information:** When you voluntarily subscribe to market updates, we collect the contact information needed to manage and deliver that subscription.

**Technical and usage information:** The website and its service providers may collect IP addresses, browser and device information, referring pages, pages visited, interaction events, and similar information needed for analytics, website operation, and security.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, financial account information, identification documents, or other sensitive information by email.

## How we use information

Information may be used to:

- Respond to your commercial real estate inquiry and discuss possible next steps.
- Evaluate space requirements, property questions, or requested brokerage services.
- Maintain relevant correspondence and follow-up records.
- Send market updates when you have separately subscribed.
- Measure website usage and improve its content and performance.
- Prevent spam, maintain security, and meet applicable legal or brokerage recordkeeping obligations.

Contacting us does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails or establish a brokerage representation agreement.

## Email subscriptions

Newsletter subscriptions are managed through MailerLite. The website uses a confirmation process for new subscribers. You may unsubscribe through the link provided in marketing emails.

MailerLite may process subscription information and email engagement information, including delivery, opening, and link interaction data, depending on the settings enabled in the account.

## Cookies, analytics, and tracking

The website uses Google Analytics to help understand visitor activity and whether website content and contact options are useful.

The website may measure certain interactions, such as contact-link clicks and newsletter signup clicks.

Google Analytics and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Depending on their configurations, these services may collect information about activity over time and across websites.

## Service providers and information sharing

Information may be processed or disclosed as reasonably necessary to operate the website and respond to inquiries, including through:

- Dickson Commercial Group and authorized personnel involved in providing brokerage services.
- Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery, and security.
- Google services for website analytics and business correspondence.
- MailerLite for newsletter subscriptions and delivery.
- Email and customer relationship management systems used to organize and respond to relevant inquiries.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or as otherwise permitted under applicable legal obligations.

Third-party service providers maintain their own privacy notices. Relevant information is available from [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Retention and security

Information is retained as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements, security considerations, and brokerage recordkeeping obligations.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request corrections, or request deletion where available under applicable law.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal or brokerage obligations.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link.

Where applicable, requests concerning the sale of covered personal information may also be submitted through the contact information below.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. Material updates will be reflected by revising the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, providing additional notice.
