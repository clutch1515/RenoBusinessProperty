For many owners, the existing tenant is the most valuable part of the property. Before I advise on a renewal, I compare it with the realistic alternative, because accepting the first proposal can create long-term costs.

A smooth renewal avoids vacancy, leasing costs, and disruption. A rushed one can lock in below-market terms, defer needed work, or carry outdated lease language forward. These are the five questions I work through with owners.

## 1. When does the decision really need to be made?

Start before the lease notice date. Check the lease for renewal option deadlines, notice requirements, and how option rent is determined. The owner needs time to evaluate the tenant, market the space if necessary, scope improvements, and compare renewal economics with a realistic vacancy and re-leasing scenario. Starting early also gives the tenant time to plan, which tends to make negotiations more constructive.

## 2. How is the tenant performing?

Review payment history, use of the space, compliance, maintenance issues, customer or employee impact, communication, and any unresolved obligations. Consider the tenant’s business outlook and how important the location is to its operation. A reliable tenant may justify a different risk assessment than a chronically difficult one.

## 3. What are the credible alternatives?

Estimate achievable rent, downtime, commissions, improvements, legal cost, free rent, and the probability of securing a replacement. Compare those costs with the complete renewal proposal over the same period. If the space would need significant work to attract a new tenant, or if comparable space is readily available nearby, the renewal may be worth more than its headline rent suggests.

- How long has similar space taken to lease recently?
- What improvements would a new tenant likely need?
- What concessions are competing properties offering?
- How would a vacancy affect financing, value, or a planned sale?

## 4. What capital work is approaching?

Roof, HVAC, paving, storefront, loading, code, or accessibility work can affect the deal. Clarify which party handles each item and whether the renewal creates the right moment to complete it. Coordinating capital work with a renewal can reduce disruption and make the tenant’s commitment more durable.

## 5. Does the document still fit the property?

A renewal is an opportunity to revisit operating-expense language, insurance, maintenance, assignment, options, guarantees, use, signage, parking, access, and other provisions with qualified counsel. Leases signed years ago may not reflect current costs, current practices, or the owner’s plans for the property.

## Bringing it together

Put the renewal and the realistic alternative side by side over the same time period, including rent, concessions, improvements, commissions, downtime, and risk. That comparison usually makes the right decision clear and gives the owner a solid basis for negotiation. The same thinking applies when a property is being [prepared for a value review](/insights/prepare-commercial-property-value-review/).

## How I approach it

I like to start renewal conversations well before the notice date. Time gives you options: to evaluate the tenant, scope improvements, and test the market if needed.

## Common questions

### Should I always ask for market rent at renewal?

Market rent is a reasonable reference point, but the full comparison also includes downtime, re-leasing costs, and tenant quality. A slightly lower rent from a strong tenant can produce a better outcome than a vacancy.

### What if the tenant has a renewal option with a set rent?

The option terms control. Review how the option is exercised and how rent is determined, and plan any other negotiations around those terms.

### When should I involve a broker or attorney?

Early enough to understand the alternatives and review the lease language before negotiations begin. Last-minute renewals limit options.

### Practical takeaway

Compare the complete renewal with the complete re-leasing scenario. Headline rent alone does not tell you which choice is better.
